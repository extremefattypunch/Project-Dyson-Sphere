## A. Summary

- **Solar collection** (`helioswarm.solar`, PDF §8.1).
  - `S(r) = S_1AU (au/r_sun)²`, with `r_sun` the satellite–Sun-centre distance taken from the M3
    `IlluminationResult` (Sun from the ephemeris provider, J2000/SSB, light-time retarded).
  - Incidence: `sun_tracking` (cos = 1) or `fixed_normal` (cos = max(n·ŝ, 0)). Back-face light
    gives zero power. The M3 illumination fraction multiplies the flux.
  - `incident = S A cos f`, `absorbed = α · incident`, `reflected = (1 − α) · incident`.
    Reflected light is recorded separately and is never waste heat.
- **PV conversion** (PDF §8.2). `η_PV(T) = clip(η_ref(1 + γ_T(T − T_ref)), η_min, η_max)` is a
  labelled local linear approximation, defined relative to **absorbed** power as the M0 schema
  fixes. `P_PV = P_abs · η_PV(T)`.
  - `PvEfficiencyLaw.linear_range_K()` gives the unclipped validity interval.
  - `pv_efficiency_clipped` flags each sample where the clip is active.
- **`SolarPowerResult`.** The PDF p.4 fields plus extras: `flux_W_m2`, `incidence_cosine`,
  `illumination_fraction`, `illuminated`, `incident_W`, `reflected_W`, `absorbed_W`,
  `pv_temperature_K`, `pv_efficiency`, `pv_efficiency_clipped`, `pv_electric_W`, `pv_heat_W`.
  It is vectorised over epochs (TDB) or over distance (`epochs_tdb_s=None`).
- **On-board power chain** (`helioswarm.power_chain`). `onboard_power_chain()` is the single
  owner of `η_pc`, `η_e2b` and `η_optics_or_rf` (PDF §10.1 stages up to the aperture).
  - It returns `P_dc_to_tx`, `P_beam`, `P_aperture` and the three stage losses.
  - The M4 radiator takes its waste heat from it. M6 `link_budget` must reuse it rather than
    recompute these stages.
- **PV array thermal node** (`helioswarm.thermal`, node a). The node is a thin isothermal flat
  plate.
  - Front and back IR emissivities, and the view factor `thermal.pv_view_factor` from each face
    to deep space.
  - Balance: `P_abs − P_PV(T) = (ε_f+ε_b) σ A F (T⁴ − T_space⁴)`.
  - Solved with `scipy.optimize.brentq` on a closed-form bracket that contains every root in
    exact arithmetic. float64 effects near `T_space` (tiny `P_abs` at penumbra entry/exit) are
    handled explicitly; see the review outcomes, finding 1.
  - Reported per sample: convergence (residual ≤ 1e-12 P_abs + the float64 representation
    floor), a uniqueness condition, the bracket, the iteration count, and feasibility against
    `collector.max_operating_temperature_K` with its margin.
- **Radiator node** (node b).
  - Waste heat: `Q_waste = P_PV(1−η_pc) + P_dc(1−η_e2b) + P_beam(1−η_o) + Q_other`.
  - Geometry: a flat plate with `n_faces` = `radiating_sides`, view factor F to deep space, and
    no view of the Sun or the array.
  - Required area at the limit: `A_req = Q/(ε σ n F (T_lim⁴ − T_space⁴))`.
  - With a configured area, the equilibrium temperature is solved analytically and the result
    reports the feasibility flag, the temperature margin and the area margin.
  - Optional mass proxy.
  - `ThermalResult` carries both nodes, both verdicts, the overall `feasible` and the binding
    margin `margin_K = min(pv_margin_K, radiator_margin_K)`, with
    `feasible = pv_converged & (margin_K ≥ 0)`.
  - The PV heat `Q_PV` is rejected by the PV plate (node a), not by this radiator: an
    explicit departure from the PDF §8.3 lumped formula (F, deviation 6).
- **Energy ledger** (`energy_ledger`). Eight identities, from the collector to the spacecraft
  boundary, are checked per sample to 1e-12 of `incident + Q_other`, plus the float64
  representation floor of any solved temperature in the identity. The ledger is returned as a
  diagnostic and asserted in tests. Measured: ≤ 6.5e-16.
  - Independent checks: the PV-node identity, the collector identity and the radiator
    inventory sum.
  - Consistency checks only: the three chain identities and the radiator balance hold by
    construction or round trip, and the spacecraft identity is their sum.
  - `test_energy_ledger_from_hand_computed_terms` computes every term in closed form.
- **Required results (PDF §8.4).** `power_thermal_vs_distance(r_m, models)` evaluates arrays of
  heliocentric distance and returns, at the transmitter operating point
  `PowerThermalModels.efficiencies`:
  - flux and collector power;
  - PV efficiency and temperature;
  - waste heat and radiator area (or radiator temperature);
  - net DC power to the transmitter (`chain.dc_to_tx_W`);
  - the feasibility flags and margins.

  `power_thermal_from_illumination(ill, models)` gives the same outputs along a trajectory.

## E. Assumptions and equations implemented

The full text, with validity ranges, is in `docs/model_assumptions.md`, section "M4". In brief:

- **Irradiance.** `S = S_1AU (au/r)²`. This is exact for flux on a plane normal to the Sun
  direction outside a spherically symmetric Sun, so no finite-disk correction applies at
  normal incidence. TSI variability and O(v/c) aberration/Doppler terms are neglected.
- **Collected power.** `incident = S A cos(inc) f`; `P_abs = α · incident` (PDF §8.1);
  `reflected = (1 − α) · incident` (the array is opaque).
- **Incidence.**
  - `sun_tracking`: cos = 1.
  - `fixed_normal`: cos = max(n·ŝ, 0), using the J2000 normal and the retarded Sun direction.
  - Back-face light gives zero power, and back-face absorption is not modelled.
- **PV conversion.** `η_PV(T) = clip(η_ref(1+γ_T(T−T_ref)), η_min, η_max)` (PDF §8.2), a local
  linear approximation relative to absorbed power. `P_PV = P_abs η_PV(T)` and
  `Q_PV = P_abs − P_PV`.
- **PV node.** `P_abs − P_PV(T) = (ε_f+ε_b) σ A F (T⁴ − T_space⁴)` (Stefan–Boltzmann; σ
  CODATA 2018).
  - The bracket `[T_eq(η_hi), T_eq(η_lo)]` with `T_eq(η) = ((1−η)P_abs/G + T_space⁴)^¼` is
    derived in `thermal.py`.
  - Uniqueness holds if `γ_T ≥ 0` or `P_abs η_ref |γ_T| < 4 G T_lo³`.
- **On-board chain.** `P_dc_to_tx = P_PV η_pc`, `P_beam = P_dc η_e2b`, `P_aperture =
  P_beam η_o` (PDF §10.1). Each loss is the stage input minus its output.
- **Radiator.**
  - Waste heat: `Q_waste = Σ on-board losses + Q_other`.
  - Rejected heat: `Q_rad = ε σ A n F (T⁴ − T_space⁴)` (PDF §8.3, with
    `A_eff = A · emitting_side_factor · view_factor` and `emitting_side_factor =
    radiating_sides`).
  - Required area: `A_req = Q/(ε σ n F (T_lim⁴ − T_space⁴))`.
  - Equilibrium temperature for a given area: `T_eq = (Q/(ε σ A n F) + T_space⁴)^¼`.
- **Energy ledger.** All identities of section A, to 1e-12.

## F. Known limitations and next milestone

### Deviations from the plan / task text, and why

1. **`incidence_mode` naming.** The M0 schema calls the fixed-attitude mode `fixed_normal`, not
   `fixed_attitude`. M4 follows the schema rather than renaming a validated field.
2. **PV solver bracket.** The brief proposed `[T_space+ε, T_max_bracket]`. M4 uses a bracket
   derived in closed form from the efficiency bounds, which:
   - proves that a root exists (in exact arithmetic);
   - contains every root;
   - needs no arbitrary upper temperature.

   In float64 the bracket ends carry a few ulp of rounding, and for tiny `P_abs` the bracket
   collapses next to `T_space`. Three measures handle this: a factored `T⁴ − T_space⁴`, a
   representation-floor term in the tolerance, and a float-neighbour polish (review
   finding 1). If a sample still fails, `evaluate_power_thermal` raises instead of propagating
   NaN. Non-convergence and possible non-uniqueness are reported per sample.
3. **One new public field on an M3 result.** `IlluminationResult` gains `sun_direction_unit` and
   `frame`, so that the retarded Sun direction is computed in one place only.
4. **Power-chain module.** The on-board stage computation lives in a new small module
   `power_chain.py`, as the task suggested, so that M4 and M6 share one owner per efficiency.
   M6 `link_budget.py` must import `onboard_power_chain` and not re-implement the first three
   stages.
5. **Radiator temperature in sizing mode.** With no configured radiator area,
   `equilibrium_temperature_K` is the design temperature `T_lim` and `radiator_margin_K` is 0
   by construction. Radiator feasibility can only fail in the configured-area mode. The
   PDF-level `margin_K` is the binding margin `min(pv_margin_K, radiator_margin_K)`, so it is
   negative whenever `feasible` is false for a temperature reason (review finding 7).
6. **PV heat is not on the radiator (PDF §8.3).** PDF §8.3 writes
   `Q_waste = Q_PV + Q_transmitter + Q_power_electronics + Q_other` for one radiator. Here
   `Q_PV = P_abs − P_PV` is radiated by the PV plate (node a), and the radiator carries only the
   on-board chain losses plus `Q_other`. This follows the task's two-node design and is
   physically consistent: the cells dissipate locally, and no conductive link is modelled.
   Nothing is dropped, because the spacecraft ledger identity checks the sum (review finding 8).

### Limitations

- **Steady state at every epoch.** There is no thermal capacity, so eclipse epochs drop to
  `T_space` instantly. There are no eclipses in the MVP year.
- **Optics/RF loss on the radiator.** The whole loss is assigned to the radiator, which is
  conservative for spill-over.
- **No coupling between nodes.** Conduction and radiation between the PV array and the radiator
  are neglected.
- **Back-face sunlight.** Sunlight on the back face of a fixed-attitude panel is not absorbed in
  the model.
- **Constant surface properties.** Emissivities and absorptivity do not depend on temperature or
  wavelength.
- **Linear PV law.** It is a local approximation. A measured η(T) curve can replace
  `PvEfficiencyLaw` behind the same `efficiency()` interface.
- **Assumed defaults.** `collector.max_operating_temperature_K = 400 K` is assumed, not sourced,
  and `thermal.pv_view_factor = 1.0` is the isolated-plate (optimistic) value.

**A. PV solver floating-point floor (finding 1).** Reproduced exactly on the MVP models:

- illumination fraction 1e-13 gave `converged=False`, `feasible=False` and an open ledger
  (5.5e-10);
- 1e-15, 1e-18, 1e-25 and 5e-324 raised `RuntimeError`;
- `solve_pv_temperature` returned NaN, with a collapsed bracket, for `P_abs ≤ 5.6e-8 W`.

Fix in `thermal.py`:

1. `fourth_power_difference_K4`, `(T − T_s)(T + T_s)(T² + T_s²)`, is used in the balance, in
   `PvThermalNode.radiated_W` and in the radiator formulas.
2. `pv_balance_floor_W = (P_abs |η_ref γ_T| + 4 G T³) spacing(T)` is added to the
   convergence tolerance, both in the solver and in the `converged` flag.
3. The brentq `xtol` is now 4 ulp of `T_lo`. The old 1e-13 K was ~225 ulp at 2.7 K.
4. A float-neighbour polish (≤ 64 ulp) handles a brentq result above tolerance and bracket
   ends whose sign rounding flipped (the old `else: continue` → NaN path).
5. The ledger adds the same representation floor to the identities that contain a solved
   temperature: the PV node, the radiator balance (with a new `radiator_balance_floor_W` in
   fixed-area mode) and the spacecraft identity. It also adds the smallest normal float64 to
   every identity, which covers subnormal underflow: `Q_waste ≈ 1e-317 W` underflowed in the
   `A_req` round trip.

After the fix:

- every `P_abs` from 1e-30 to 1e8 W converges;
- fractions 1e-11 … 5e-324 converge, are feasible and close the ledger in both radiator
  modes;
- a ±1 µrad penumbra-edge sweep with the M3 overlap function at 0.3/1.0/1.2 au converges and
  closes;
- MVP numbers are unchanged (table, MVP year, ledger 3.9e-16 / 5.5e-16).

New tests, which check the temperature against a 50-digit `Decimal` fixed-point root to 2 ulp,
not just "converged":

- `test_tiny_illumination_fraction_converges_and_closes` (2 radiator modes);
- `test_penumbra_edge_sweep_never_aborts`;
- `test_fourth_power_difference_has_no_cancellation`;
- `test_root_a_few_ulp_off_is_polished`.

Docstrings and `model_assumptions.md` no longer claim that existence holds unconditionally;
they say it holds in exact arithmetic and describe the float64 handling.

**B. SPICE flux reference (finding 3).** Confirmed. Over the 8767 epochs the module flux
deviates from an instantaneous-Sun reference by 5.87e-8, and from a 69.184 s epoch-shifted Sun
by 6.69e-8; both pass rtol 1e-6. The fixture now computes the retarded Sun independently: three
fixed-point steps of `t_e = t − |r_sat − r_sun(t_e)|/c` with `eph.positions`. The flux is
asserted against it to 1e-12 (measured 5.6e-16). The instantaneous-Sun deviation is asserted
to be > 1e-8 (retardation really applied) and < 1e-7 (the documented bound). The mutation
"instantaneous Sun for illumination" is now caught by this test.

**C. Solver failure reporting (finding 4).** Confirmed: no test exercised these paths. Four
monkeypatch tests replace `helioswarm.thermal.brentq` (see the sketch after this list):

- an unconverged midpoint gives NaN, `converged=False`, the iteration count, and
  `evaluate_power_thermal` raising `RuntimeError`;
- an unconverged but correct root is still discarded (the guard is now discriminating: the
  "non-convergence ignored" mutation is caught);
- a "converged" wrong root (the bracket end) gives NaN and not converged;
- a root 10 ulp off at `P_abs = 1e-5 W` is polished back to ≤ 1 ulp.

The stand-in is a module-level patch, sketched here for the unconverged-midpoint case (the test
file's `_fake_brentq` covers all four):

```python
monkeypatch.setattr(
    thermal_module,
    "brentq",
    lambda f, a, b, **kw: (0.5 * (a + b), SimpleNamespace(converged=False, iterations=7)),
)
```

**D. Area scaling (finding 5).** Confirmed: area was fixed at 1e4 m² in all tests.
`test_area_scaling_at_fixed_everything_else` compares A and 3.7 A over 0.3–1.2 au. `T_pv` is
unchanged, and `P_PV`, `Q_waste` and `A_req` scale by 3.7, all to 1e-14; the measured rounding
is ≤ 4.4e-16.

**E. Ledger independence (finding 6).** Confirmed as stated: the chain identities hold by
construction, the radiator balance is a round trip, and the spacecraft identity is their sum.
The `EnergyLedger` docstring, the module docstring, `model_assumptions.md`, section A and the

and the inventory sum. `test_energy_ledger_from_hand_computed_terms` computes every ledger

the hand closure and the module's terms.

**F. Binding margin (finding 7).** Confirmed: at 0.3 au, `feasible=False` with `margin_K=0.0`.
Option 1 was implemented:

- `ThermalResult.radiator_margin_K` is the radiator margin (0 by construction when sized at
  the limit);
- `margin_K = min(pv_margin_K, radiator_margin_K)` is the PDF p.4 / §8.4 margin to the
  temperature limit;
- `feasible == pv_converged & (margin_K ≥ 0)`, tested over 46 distances in three radiator
  modes (`test_feasible_flag_and_binding_margin_agree`).

MVP at 0.3 au now reports `margin_K = −158.8 K`. Existing tests were moved to
`radiator_margin_K` where they meant the radiator. No later-milestone code used `margin_K`.

**H. Reproducible commands (finding 9).** Confirmed. Section C now gives the complete
commands, with the outputs from the review re-run: the scipy signature, the hash re-pin, the
MVP table and the MVP year. The mutation script is archived as `scripts/mutation_check_m4.py`.
Archiving it exposed a flaw in the original scratchpad script, which classified any non-zero
pytest exit as "caught". Run naively, a pytest usage error (`-p no:cacheprovider` breaks the
`cache_dir` config) made every mutation "caught" with "no tests ran". The archived script
counts only exit 1, meaning tests ran and failed. With it, three of the seven new mutations
initially survived: polish dropped, unfactored scalar `T⁴ − T_s⁴`, and brentq non-convergence
ignored. Discriminating tests were added for each (C and A). The final run on the final source
catches all 18 with exit 0. `validation_report.md` now cites the script.
